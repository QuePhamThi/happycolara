Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all pictures?
The first 3 pictures of every world are free. HappyColara Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 197 pictures. Tap the lock button on the home screen, answer the grown-up question, then open Purchases.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it back?
Open the Parents area (lock button) → Purchases → Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Family Sharing members get Pro too.

How do I set a daily play-time limit?
Parents area → Play time. When the limit is reached, a break screen appears and only a grown-up can add more time.

How do I save or share a picture?
Open My Artwork, choose a picture and tap Save / Share. A grown-up question appears first.

Where are my child's pictures stored?
Only on the device, inside the app. Deleting the app removes them.
