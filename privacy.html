Privacy Policy

HappyColara · Last updated: November 9, 2026

HappyColara is made for children ages 3–10. We take their privacy seriously.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It does not ask for names, ages, email addresses, photos, location or contacts.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no login, no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Your child's artwork

Pictures and coloring progress are saved only on this device, inside the app. They are included in your normal device backup (iCloud or computer backup) if you use one, under your Apple account.

Sharing

A picture leaves the app only when a grown-up answers the parental gate question and chooses to export or share it using the iPhone/iPad share sheet. The app has no access to where you send it.

Purchases

The app offers one optional in-app purchase, HappyColara Pro, which unlocks all pictures. It is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID; the app never sees your payment details. Buying and restoring are only possible after answering the parental gate.

Internet

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Deleting data

Parents can delete all artwork and rewards in the Parents area, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in an app update and shown here.

Contact

For any privacy question, send us a message.
